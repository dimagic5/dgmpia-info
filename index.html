DGMPIA

A personal investment research and monitoring application, developed and operated by the owner of the dimagic5 GitHub account.

Purpose

DGMPIA collects market information, runs analytical methods and simulations, monitors watchlists, and produces notifications for its operator. It is a private experimental application, not a public investment service.

Optional email integration

The operator may connect a personal Google or Microsoft account using OAuth to send application notifications and test messages to recipients configured by the operator. Email integration is optional and requires the account owner's authorization.

For Google, the integration requests permission to identify the connected account and send email through Gmail. It does not request permission to read the inbox.

Local operation

The application runs in an environment controlled by its operator. This website contains public information only; it does not provide access to the application or receive OAuth callbacks.

Privacy and contact

See the privacy policy for information about account data, permissions and revocation. Project contact: GitHub issues. Do not post credentials, tokens, email contents or other private information in public issues.
