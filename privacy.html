DGMPIA · Privacy policy

Privacy policy

This policy describes the optional email integration of DGMPIA, a personal application operated by the owner of the dimagic5 GitHub account.

Information used

When an account is connected, DGMPIA processes the account identifier and email address, OAuth access and refresh tokens, configured recipient addresses, and the subject and body of outgoing notifications. Delivery records and technical errors may be retained for troubleshooting.

Google permissions

DGMPIA uses basic identity permissions (openid and email) to identify the connected account, and the Gmail gmail.send permission to send notifications and test messages explicitly configured or requested by the operator. This integration does not request Gmail inbox-reading permissions.

Purpose and sharing

Account data is used to maintain the authorized connection and send application email. Necessary data is transmitted to Google or Microsoft to perform the requested operation, and outgoing message contents are delivered to the configured recipients and their email providers. Google account data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and protection

Account configuration and authorization credentials are stored in the operator-controlled application environment. Stored refresh tokens are encrypted by the application; the operator is responsible for protecting encryption keys, the host, database and backups. This public information website does not store those credentials.

Retention and deletion

Connection data is retained while the integration is configured or needed for its operation. The operator can remove the configured account and associated stored credentials from the application environment. Previously sent messages remain in the relevant mail systems under their respective retention rules; database backups and delivery logs must be handled separately by the operator.

Revoke access

You can revoke Google authorization at any time through Google Account connections. Microsoft authorization can be managed through your Microsoft account permissions. Revocation prevents further use of that authorization but does not itself delete local configuration or previously delivered messages.

Google API Limited Use

DGMPIA's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This website and contact

These static pages contain no application analytics, tracking scripts or forms. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages, which may process technical request data under GitHub's privacy statement.

For questions, use the project issues page without disclosing private information. The support email configured in Google's consent screen is another contact for the application.
